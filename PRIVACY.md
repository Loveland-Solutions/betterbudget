# BetterBudget Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 14, 2026
**Last updated:** October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Loveland Solutions, LLC ("we," "us" or "our") handles information in connection with the **BetterBudget** app (the "App"), available on the Apple App Store, Google Play and the Microsoft Store.

**The short version:** BetterBudget does not collect your personal information. Everything you enter into the App stays on your device, or in a folder you choose yourself. The App never connects to the internet. We have no accounts, no servers that store your data, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## 2. Information the App does not collect

BetterBudget is designed to work entirely on your device. The App makes no network connections of its own. We do **not** collect, receive, store or have access to:

- The budgets, transactions, amounts, categories, notes or any other information you enter into the App
- Your name, email address, phone number or postal address
- Your bank, card or financial account information
- Your location
- Your contacts, photos, camera or microphone
- Device identifiers, advertising identifiers or usage analytics

The App does not include analytics tools, advertising networks, social media plug-ins or other third-party tracking code.

## 3. Information stored on your device

Your budget data is stored on your device, in a file encrypted with a key that the App keeps in your device's secure storage (the Keychain on Apple devices, the Keystore on Android, and the equivalent on Windows). When you set up the App, it gives you a recovery key. We never see that key and cannot recover it, so keep it somewhere safe. You control your data:

- **Bank files you import:** If you import a statement file from your bank, the App reads it on your device. The file is never sent anywhere.
- **A record of recent problems:** When something goes wrong in the App, for example a file it couldn't read or a change it couldn't save, it keeps a short record on your device: when it happened, the message it showed you, the technical reason behind it, and the App's version. It keeps the last 100 problems from the past 90 days. The record is not encrypted, so that it can still be read when your ledger won't open; for that reason it holds no amounts, balances or transactions. A message or technical reason can include the name or location of a file or folder, which may contain your device's user name. The App never sends the record anywhere. You can see it in Settings on a computer, or at the foot of the Budget screen on a phone, where **Copy all** copies it for you to paste into a bug report yourself. Deleting the App removes it.
- **A folder you choose:** If you choose to store your ledger in a folder that a cloud service keeps in sync, such as Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or Box, the App saves an encrypted copy of your ledger in that folder, and the App on your other computers and phones reads and writes files there. The App itself does not connect to that service: the service's own software on your device uploads the folder. The copy is encrypted, but the service stores it under your account and its own privacy policy, not ours. The ledger includes the names you have given your computers and phones, so the App can show where each transaction was entered. We cannot access the folder.
- **Device backups:** If you back up your device (for example, through iCloud, Time Machine, Google, or Windows/OneDrive backup), your App data may be included in those backups. Backups are controlled by you and governed by the privacy policy of the backup provider, not by us. We cannot access them.
- **Backups and files you save:** When you back up your ledger to a file or save a file from the App, that file goes only where you choose to save or send it.
- **Deleting the App** removes the data stored in the App's own storage on most devices. On a computer, the operating system may leave the App's data behind until you delete it yourself. Copies in folders you chose, and backup files you saved, stay until you delete them.

Because we never receive your budget data, we cannot view, recover, correct or delete it for you. Please keep your own backups and your recovery key if your data is important to you.

## 4. Downloads

BetterBudget is free, with nothing to buy inside it. You download it through the Apple App Store, Google Play or the Microsoft Store. The store handles the download and may collect information to provide it, such as your store account. That information is governed by the store's privacy policy:

- Apple: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- Google: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- Microsoft: [privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement)

There is nothing to pay, so we never receive payment information.

The stores give developers aggregated download reports (for example, the number of downloads by country). If you have chosen in your device settings to share crash or diagnostic reports with app developers, the store may also give us those reports. We use this information only to understand how many people use the App and to fix problems with it, and we do not use it to identify you.

## 5. When you contact us

If you email us at **contact@lls.mn** for support or with a question, we receive:

- Your email address
- Your name, if it appears in your email
- Anything you choose to include in your message

We use this information **only to answer you** and to help with your request. We do not add you to a mailing list, use your information for marketing, or sell it.

Our email is provided by Google Workspace (Google LLC), which processes messages on our behalf. Google's privacy policy is at [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We keep support messages for up to 24 months after your request is resolved, then delete them, unless we need to keep them longer to comply with the law. You can ask us to delete your messages sooner at any time.

Please do not send us your budget data, account numbers or other sensitive financial information by email.

**Bug reports and ideas through our website:** You can also report a bug or suggest an idea through the contact form on our website, at [lls.mn/en/contact](https://lls.mn/en/contact/). The form asks for your name, your email address and your message, and for your agreement to the website's privacy policy, at [lls.mn/en/privacy](https://lls.mn/en/privacy/), which explains how the website and the services that run it handle what you send. In the App, **Send feedback…**, at the foot of the sidebar on a computer or beside Accounts at the top of the Budget screen on a phone, and on the screens shown when your ledger won't open, opens that form in your web browser with only the App's version and your device's operating system filled in, never your transactions or balances. The form shows you both before you send it, and nothing is sent until you send it yourself; the App makes no connection of its own. To send us the details of a problem, the App's **Copy** and **Copy all** buttons put them on your clipboard for you to paste into the form; read them first, since they may include the name or location of a file. Please do not paste your budget data into the form.

## 6. How we share information

**We do not sell your personal information.** We also do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as that term is defined under California law.

The only personal information we ever receive is what you send us by email or through our website's contact form (Section 5). We also share it if we are required to by law, regulation or court order.

## 8. Security

The App does not transmit your budget data anywhere, which is the strongest protection we can offer. The data the App stores, including any copy it saves in a folder you choose, is encrypted. Keeping your device secure (for example, with a passcode and up-to-date software) helps protect the data stored on it.

We protect support messages with reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections and secured business accounts.

## 9. Your rights

Because the App does not collect personal information, most privacy rights apply only to support messages you have sent us. To exercise any of the rights below, email **contact@lls.mn**. We will respond within the time required by applicable law (generally within 30 days, or 45 days for California requests). We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

### 9.1 European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland (GDPR)

For support messages, we are the controller. Our legal basis is our **legitimate interest** in responding to people who contact us (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), or **steps you have asked us to take** in connection with your use of the App (Article 6(1)(b)).

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or receive a portable copy of your personal information, and to object to our processing of it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority where you live or work.

### 9.2 California (CCPA/CPRA and CalOPPA)

If you are a California resident:

- **Categories collected:** In the past 12 months, the only personal information we have collected is **identifiers** (such as your email address and name) and the contents of messages, and only when you emailed us or used our website's contact form. The source is you, and the purpose is responding to your request.
- **No sale or sharing:** We have not sold or shared personal information in the past 12 months, and we do not sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.
- **No sensitive personal information:** We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by the CPRA.
- **Your rights:** You have the right to know what personal information we have collected about you, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of its sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof of the agent's authority and verify your identity with you directly.

Under the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA): the App does not track you over time or across third-party websites or apps, so it does not respond differently to "Do Not Track" signals. No third parties collect personal information through the App. We will notify you of changes to this policy as described in Section 11.

### 9.3 Mongolia

Under Mongolia's Law on the Protection of Personal Information, you have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to receive a copy of it, and to have it corrected or deleted. The only personal information we hold is the support messages you sent us (Section 5). You may also complain to the National Human Rights Commission of Mongolia.
