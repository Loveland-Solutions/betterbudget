# BetterBudget Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 14, 2026
**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Loveland Solutions, LLC ("we," "us" or "our") handles information in connection with the **BetterBudget** app (the "App"), available on the Apple App Store, Google Play and the Microsoft Store.

**The short version:** BetterBudget does not collect your personal information. Everything you enter into the App stays on your device, or in a folder you choose yourself. The App never connects to the internet. We have no accounts, no servers that store your data, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## 2. Information the App does not collect

BetterBudget is designed to work entirely on your device. The App makes no network connections of its own. We do **not** collect, receive, store or have access to:

- The budgets, transactions, amounts, categories, notes or any other information you enter into the App
- Your name, email address, phone number or postal address
- Your bank, card or financial account information
- Your location
- Your contacts, photos, camera or microphone
- Device identifiers, advertising identifiers or usage analytics

The App does not include analytics tools, advertising networks, social media plug-ins or other third-party tracking code.

## 3. Information stored on your device

Your budget data is stored on your device, in a file encrypted with a key that the App keeps in your device's secure storage (the Keychain on Apple devices, the Keystore on Android, and the equivalent on Windows). When you set up the App, it gives you a recovery key. We never see that key and cannot recover it, so keep it somewhere safe. You control your data:

- **Bank files you import:** If you import a statement file from your bank, the App reads it on your device. The file is never sent anywhere.
- **A folder you choose:** If you choose to store your ledger in a folder that a cloud service keeps in sync, such as Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or Box, the App saves an encrypted copy of your ledger in that folder, and the App on your other computers and phones reads and writes files there. The App itself does not connect to that service: the service's own software on your device uploads the folder. The copy is encrypted, but the service stores it under your account and its own privacy policy, not ours. The ledger includes the names you have given your computers and phones, so the App can show where each transaction was entered. We cannot access the folder.
- **Device backups:** If you back up your device (for example, through iCloud, Time Machine, Google, or Windows/OneDrive backup), your App data may be included in those backups. Backups are controlled by you and governed by the privacy policy of the backup provider, not by us. We cannot access them.
- **Backups and files you save:** When you back up your ledger to a file or save a file from the App, that file goes only where you choose to save or send it.
- **Deleting the App** removes the data stored in the App's own storage on most devices. On a computer, the operating system may leave the App's data behind until you delete it yourself. Copies in folders you chose, and backup files you saved, stay until you delete them.

Because we never receive your budget data, we cannot view, recover, correct or delete it for you. Please keep your own backups and your recovery key if your data is important to you.

You buy BetterBudget, and any in-app purchases, through the Apple App Store, Google Play or the Microsoft Store. The store handles your payment and collects the information needed to complete it, such as your account, payment method and billing details. That information is governed by the store's privacy policy:

- Apple: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- Google: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- Microsoft: [privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement)

We do not receive your payment card details, billing address or other payment information.

The stores give developers aggregated sales and download reports (for example, the number of purchases by country). If you have chosen in your device settings to share crash or diagnostic reports with app developers, the store may also give us those reports. We use this information only to understand sales and to fix problems with the App, and we do not use it to identify you.

## 9. Your rights

Because the App does not collect personal information, most privacy rights apply only to support emails you have sent us. To exercise any of the rights below, email **contact@lls.mn**. We will respond within the time required by applicable law (generally within 30 days, or 45 days for California requests). We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

### 9.1 European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland (GDPR)

For support emails, we are the controller. Our legal basis is our **legitimate interest** in responding to people who contact us (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), or **steps you have asked us to take** in connection with your purchase (Article 6(1)(b)).

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or receive a portable copy of your personal information, and to object to our processing of it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority where you live or work.

### 9.2 California (CCPA/CPRA and CalOPPA)

If you are a California resident:

- **Categories collected:** In the past 12 months, the only personal information we have collected is **identifiers** (such as your email address and name) and the contents of messages, and only when you emailed us. The source is you, and the purpose is responding to your request.
- **No sale or sharing:** We have not sold or shared personal information in the past 12 months, and we do not sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.
- **No sensitive personal information:** We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by the CPRA.
- **Your rights:** You have the right to know what personal information we have collected about you, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of its sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof of the agent's authority and verify your identity with you directly.

Under the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA): the App does not track you over time or across third-party websites or apps, so it does not respond differently to "Do Not Track" signals. No third parties collect personal information through the App. We will notify you of changes to this policy as described in Section 11.

### 9.3 Mongolia

Under Mongolia's Law on the Protection of Personal Information, you have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to receive a copy of it, and to have it corrected or deleted. The only personal information we hold is the support email you sent us (Section 5). You may also complain to the National Human Rights Commission of Mongolia.
